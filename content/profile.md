## Bio

I'm Timothée (Timothy) Kabwe Zumbi, a PMP®-certified Project Analyst based in Kolwezi with 5+ years in the Copperbelt mining industry at Kamoa Copper SA (Ivanhoe Mines group). My focus sits at the intersection of project data consolidation, project controls, performance analysis and management reporting.

Day to day, I track cost, schedule, progress and procurement across multiple concurrent site infrastructure projects, and build Power BI, Power Query and Excel dashboards that turn raw project and portfolio data into variance analysis, trend analysis and clear exceptions for leadership. I work daily in MS Project, handle core scheduling tasks in Primavera P6, hold basic SAP knowledge, and am currently pursuing an MBA in Data Science Management.

I also teach and lead community initiatives. As a PMI Authorized Training Partner Instructor (PMP® & CAPM®), a Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT), and Volunteer Project Manager & Certification Manager for the PMI DRC Chapter, I help professionals and teams strengthen their project governance and data skills. I work fluently in French, English and Swahili.
